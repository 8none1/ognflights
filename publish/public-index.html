Moving around the 3D view

  • Left-click and drag to pan and spin the view around.
  • Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in and out. Right-click and drag up or down also zooms, or pinch on a trackpad.
  • Hold Ctrl and left-click and drag (or middle-click and drag) to tilt the camera and change the viewing angle.
